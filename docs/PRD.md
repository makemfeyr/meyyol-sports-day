# Meyyol's Sports Day — Product Requirements Document

**Status:** Phase 1.8 MVP scope baseline; F-12 was approved on 8 November 2026 and the approved retention details were reconciled on 10 November 2026. Target behavior is not implemented.\
**Decision authority:** FD-01 through FD-25 in FOUNDER_DECISIONS.md remain canonical. The separately approved F-12 Operational Security & Readiness baseline supplements them; it is not an FD identifier. OPEN_DECISIONS.md contains unresolved operational/technical choices and unapproved recommendations.

## 1. Product vision

Meyyol's Sports Day helps schools organize sports-day events, manage houses and participants, record scores, and publish trustworthy results. Each school has a private management space. Visitors see only information that has been published and permitted for public display.

The MVP is planned as a multi-tenant SaaS product. A school must not read or change another school's operational data. The target is a server-first Next.js application backed by Supabase Auth, PostgreSQL, and Storage. The current repository remains a starter application.

## 2. Product goals

- Let an approved school configure and manage its events.
- Give Event Admins authority within their own school and no other school.
- Give Scorekeepers event-scoped access with independent score-entry and publication permissions and minimum participant data.
- Preserve result versions, correction/withdrawal reasons, and privileged-action audit history.
- Calculate house standings using the approved school configuration and competition ranking rules.
- Publish Public or Unlisted events without exposing drafts, withdrawn values, or unapproved participant names.
- Provide approved participant import and retention workflows before real participant data is used.
- Verify tenant isolation and operational readiness before the pilot.

## 3. Target users

- **Event Admins:** administer one school, including participants, events, assignments, scoring, results, and in-scope audit history.
- **Scorekeepers:** work only in assigned events in their school, with independently granted entry and publication permissions.
- **Superadmins:** review school applications and conduct defined platform operations. They do not gain ambient school-data access.
- **Public Viewers:** view published, permitted information from Public events or direct links to Unlisted events.

## 4. Roles and school membership

A user has at most one active school membership across all schools. One active membership may carry both Event Admin and Scorekeeper roles for the same school. Platform Superadmin authority is a separate grant. A Superadmin needs a separate active school membership and appropriate school role to perform ordinary school operations.

Event Admin authority applies to the member's own school. Scorekeeper access is limited to assigned events, and each assignment has separate can_enter_scores and can_publish_results flags. Public/Unlisted visibility does not grant operational access.

## 5. MVP scope

### Included

- School registration review and approval by a Superadmin.
- Designation or verification of the first Event Admin and activation only after verification.
- School-level Event Admin and Scorekeeper membership, with one active membership per user.
- School setup, events, houses, categories, competitions, participants, and in-app Scorekeeper assignments.
- Participant ID generated by the system; required Participant Name and Sports House; optional Participant Number; Year/Form and Gender only when required for competition categories. No date of birth or national ID.
- Manual participant entry and one standard Meyyol CSV template shared across schools, with validation, preview, duplicate review, row-level errors, and import summaries.
- The common CSV uses participant_name, participant_number, sports_house, year_form, and gender; participant_id is generated and excluded. Duplicate matching follows FD-17 normalization and is manually reviewed; flagged rows are never automatically inserted. Valid non-duplicate rows may proceed.
- One standard scoring configuration per school for all its events. Event Admin can configure medal and participation points. Individual and team medal awards and participation eligibility follow FD-24. No event-level overrides.
- Draft entry, authorized publication, Event Admin-only correction and withdrawal of published results, mandatory reasons, version history, and reviewed republishing.
- Public and Unlisted event portals. Public events may appear in listings; Unlisted events require a direct link and are omitted from listings. Private mode is deferred.
- Per-event public participant-name control show_participant_names, default false. Scorekeepers see only participant information needed for their assigned events.
- Audit of all privileged actions, including assignment changes and cancellations.
- Manual communication via existing channels plus in-app assignment management. Automated email, push, and in-app notifications are deferred.
- Participant/operational data has 90-day Active Retention from the official Sports Day end date, subject to approved extensions, early deletion, and a 90-day Restricted Recovery period ending in permanent deletion, except for separately retained records and legal/privacy requirements. The participant name and approved result fields in the official competition-results archive are retained for at least two years under FD-18; participant number and other operational participant fields remain under the operational lifecycle unless separately justified and approved. This archive retention does not authorize public disclosure. Emergency Recovery follows FD-18.
- Public portal viewing of published official results.

### Out of scope for the MVP

- Private or authenticated event visibility; Unlisted links are shareable and are not confidential.
- Event-specific scoring overrides.
- PDF or CSV exports. CSV participant import is in scope.
- Automated email, push, or in-app notification delivery.
- Cross-school Scorekeeper assignments.
- Superadmin access to school data merely by virtue of platform authority.
- Date of birth and national ID collection.

## 6. Core user journeys

1. **School onboarding:** the school submits an application. A Superadmin approves it, designates or verifies the first Event Admin account, and activates Event Admin access only after verification is complete.
2. **Event setup:** an Event Admin maintains school information, houses, events, categories, competitions, participants, and the school's scoring configuration.
3. **Scorekeeper assignment:** an Event Admin assigns a same-school Scorekeeper to one or more events, sets score-entry and publication permissions independently, and manages changes or cancellations in-app. Those actions are audited.
4. **Participant entry:** an Event Admin enters a participant or uploads the common CSV template. The system validates and previews rows, applies FD-17 normalization and duplicate matching, holds potential duplicates for Event Admin review, and allows valid non-duplicate rows to proceed. participant_id is generated and is not supplied in the file.
5. **Scoring and publication:** an authorized user records scores. Event Admins may publish; a Scorekeeper may publish only when the requested event assignment grants can_publish_results. Only valid published results contribute to official standings.
6. **Public viewing:** a visitor opens a Public event from the directory or an Unlisted event by direct link. The visitor sees published information only. Participant names are omitted when that event has show_participant_names=false (the default); when true, names may be displayed for that event, independently of event visibility.
7. **Correction or withdrawal:** an Event Admin provides a required reason. The system preserves result versions and audit history. A withdrawn result leaves the leaderboard immediately and visitors see a review notice without the withdrawn value or private reason. Only an Event Admin may confirm republishing after review.
8. **Retention and recovery:** the 90-day Active Retention period starts at the official Sports Day end date, with up to two pre-expiry extensions of seven days each if approved. An ordinary early-deletion request needs no Superadmin approval; when processed, Event Admin access is revoked and the 90-day Restricted Recovery period starts. Emergency Recovery follows FD-18 and is time-limited and scope-limited.

## 7. Functional requirements

- **FR-01 — School activation:** approval and first Event Admin designation/verification precede activation.
- **FR-02 — Membership:** a user has at most one active school membership; that membership may hold both local roles. Platform administration remains separate.
- **FR-03 — School administration:** Event Admins manage only their own school's operational data.
- **FR-04 — Event assignment:** Scorekeeper-only access is limited to assigned events in the member's school.
- **FR-05 — Independent permissions:** score entry and publication are distinct assignment permissions; neither implies the other.
- **FR-06 — Participants:** generate Participant ID; require name and sports house; allow an optional participant number; collect Year/Form and Gender only when required by competition categories; do not collect date of birth or national ID.
- **FR-07 — Participant privacy:** Event Admin manages participants and the show_participant_names setting for events in their own school under FD-02. Scorekeepers see the minimum information needed for their assigned events. Each event has show_participant_names, default false; when false, Public Viewers do not see participant names. When true, names may be displayed for that event, independently of Public/Unlisted visibility. The exact Scorekeeper field allowlist remains open under OD-13.
- **FR-08 — CSV:** use one standard Meyyol template for all schools. Validate before insertion and provide preview, row-level error feedback, and import totals.
- **FR-09 — CSV duplicates:** trim leading/trailing whitespace; compare names and houses case-insensitively; treat participant numbers as text and preserve leading zeros. When both records have numbers, match normalized name + number + house; when neither has a number, match name + house; when only one has a number, matching name + house is a manual-review duplicate. Never automatically insert flagged rows. Allow valid non-duplicates to proceed; Event Admin decides how flagged records proceed.
- **FR-10 — School scoring:** one school-wide configuration applies to every event in that school. Event Admin can configure medal and participation points; no event-level override exists. Individual medal winners each contribute configured medal points. A team medal result contributes once per winning house/event result, not per member.
- **FR-11 — Scoring history:** audit configuration changes and preserve the configuration/version and awarded outcome used by each historical result. A version applies according to its approved effective date; historical results are not recalculated silently.
- **FR-12 — Leaderboard:** count valid published results only; order by total points, gold, silver, then bronze, all descending. Equal four-value totals share rank and following positions skip, e.g. 1, 1, 3.
- **FR-13 — Result lifecycle:** publication is allowed for Event Admin or explicitly permitted Scorekeeper. Correction, withdrawal, and republishing of a previously published result are Event Admin-only.
- **FR-14 — Correction:** require a reason and preserve before/after values, actor, timestamp, and result version.
- **FR-15 — Withdrawal:** require a reason, preserve history, exclude the result immediately from the leaderboard, and show a public review notice without disclosing the withdrawn value. Republish requires Event Admin review and confirmation.
- **FR-16 — Public portal:** Public events may be listed; Unlisted events are omitted from listings and available by direct link. Both expose only published, permitted data. Private mode is not supported.
- **FR-17 — Auditability:** audit all privileged actions, including onboarding, role and assignment changes/cancellations, scoring changes, import finalization, result lifecycle operations, retention approvals, and emergency recovery.
- **FR-18 — Retention:** Active Retention is 90 days from the official Sports Day end date, with at most two pre-expiry extensions of up to seven days each, each requiring Superadmin approval (maximum 104 days). An ordinary early-deletion request needs no Superadmin approval; when processed it immediately revokes Event Admin access and starts 90-day Restricted Recovery. At normal expiry, the same restricted period starts. Permanently delete covered data when recovery ends, subject to separately retained records and legal/privacy requirements. Keep the official results archive for at least two years, including school, event/date, event/category, participant name, sports house, placing, medal, points, and relevant correction/withdrawal history. Do not retain participant number or other operational participant fields in that archive unless separately justified and approved. This retention exception does not change event-scoped show_participant_names (OFF by default), published-only allowlisted public projections, Public/Unlisted visibility, or private operational tables. Retain Audit Trail records for at least two years, Security Logs for 12 months, and routine Operational/Diagnostic Logs for 90 days, all separately from participant retention. Incident/investigation records may be retained longer under a controlled process; every extension is audited. Keep scoring snapshots and CSV import audit metadata separately identifiable; their durations and detailed handling remain open under OD-17.
- **FR-19 — Communication:** manage assignments in-app; use manual external communication. Automated notification delivery is outside MVP.
- **FR-20 — Exports:** CSV/PDF export is outside MVP; published official results remain viewable in the portal.

## 8. Non-functional requirements

- **Security and isolation:** server-side authorization is authoritative; PostgreSQL RLS is a second boundary. Every tenant-owned read/write is scoped to active membership and, for Scorekeeper-only access, active assignment and required permission.
- **Least privilege:** explicitly manage Data API exposure, database grants, and RLS. UI visibility is not authorization. Never ship Supabase secret/service-role credentials to a browser.
- **Session security:** use verified server-side identity and current membership/assignment state. Do not rely on stale role claims or client-supplied school/event IDs.
- **Integrity:** publication, correction, withdrawal, import finalization, scoring-version activation, participant early-deletion transitions, Emergency Recovery grants/extensions/expiry, retention approvals, and audit insertion must be atomic wherever invariants span records.
- **Privacy:** minimize student/participant data, keep roster data private, and apply the separate public-name control. Complete privacy and legal review before pilot. This document makes no claim of PDPA compliance.
- **Cache safety:** private responses are not shared across users or schools. Public cache includes only sanitized permitted output. Withdrawals must not serve stale official values.
- **Resilience:** agree data region, backup frequency, recovery objectives, monitoring, restore testing, and incident procedures before pilot.
- **Abuse controls:** rate-limit authentication, imports, and public endpoints; bound upload size and row count; validate input server-side.
- **Operational security (F-12):** require role-appropriate MFA, account and session controls, security monitoring, rate limits, incident response, protected backups and restore drills, environment separation, dependency/vulnerability management, privacy request handling, data classification, security ownership, and pre-pilot testing and sign-off. Exact implementation settings and provider capabilities remain open under OD-19 through OD-25.
- **Accessibility and usability:** provide understandable import review, result change, retention, and recovery states. Detailed accessibility targets remain for pilot planning.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

AC-01 through AC-25 preserve the original approved acceptance criteria. AC-26 through AC-48 retain the previously approved Phase 1.6 criteria and include Phase 1.7 refinements; AC-49 through AC-53 add the Phase 1.7 scoring and Emergency Recovery criteria; AC-54 through AC-82 record the Phase 1.8 F-12 operational security and readiness baseline. A criterion is a target, not evidence that the starter repository implements it. Criteria marked with an OD are not fully implementation-ready until that decision is resolved.

### Existing criteria

- **AC-01:** School application must be approved before school activation.
- **AC-02:** One user may have only one active school membership in the MVP.
- **AC-03:** Event Admin can access only their own school's management data.
- **AC-04:** Scorekeeper can access assigned events only.
- **AC-05:** Scorekeeper cannot publish without explicit permission.
- **AC-06:** Scorekeeper with publication permission can publish a valid result.
- **AC-07:** Cross-school access must be denied.
- **AC-08:** Public viewers can access published, explicitly permitted information only.
- **AC-09:** Unlisted events must not appear in public directory listings.
- **AC-10:** Result corrections preserve version history.
- **AC-11:** Critical actions record actor and timestamp.
- **AC-12:** Leaderboard is calculated from valid published results.
- **AC-13:** Revoked membership or permission prevents subsequent unauthorized actions.
- **AC-14:** Caching must not cause cross-school or cross-user data leakage.
- **AC-15:** Critical database operations are atomic across all records in their invariant.
- **AC-16:** Required RLS and authorization tests pass; no such product test suite is evidenced in the current repository.
- **AC-17:** One Scorekeeper may be assigned to multiple events within the same school.
- **AC-18:** Scorekeeper cannot correct published results.
- **AC-19:** Scorekeeper cannot withdraw published results.
- **AC-20:** Event Admin can correct published results with a mandatory reason and version history.
- **AC-21:** Event Admin can withdraw published results; withdrawn results are excluded immediately from the official leaderboard and visitors see a review notice.
- **AC-22:** Participant management supports manual entry and CSV import.
- **AC-23:** CSV import uses validation, preview, duplicate review, row-level errors, and successful/failed summaries as defined in FD-17.
- **AC-24:** Each school can configure its own standard scoring system.
- **AC-25:** Scoring configuration changes do not silently alter historical results; the applied configuration/version and outcome remain attributable.

### Phase 1.6 and Phase 1.7 criteria

- **AC-26:** Creating a participant produces a system-generated Participant ID.
- **AC-27:** Participant Name and Sports House are required; Participant Number is optional; Year/Form and Gender are collected only when required by competition categories. Date of birth and national ID are not collected in the MVP.
- **AC-28:** A Scorekeeper can see only the minimum participant fields needed for assigned events. The exact field allowlist remains open under OD-13.
- **AC-29:** Each event has a show_participant_names setting defaulting to false, which the Event Admin manages for their own school under FD-02. When false, Public Viewers see no participant names; when true, names may be displayed for that event, independent of Public/Unlisted visibility.
- **AC-30:** The common CSV template contains participant_name (required), participant_number (optional), sports_house (required), year_form (conditional), and gender (conditional). participant_id is generated and is not supplied in CSV. Encoding, delimiter, limits, and unexpected-column behavior remain open under OD-14.
- **AC-31:** CSV matching trims leading/trailing whitespace; compares names and houses case-insensitively; treats participant numbers as text; and preserves leading zeros. If both numbers exist, name + number + house match flags a potential duplicate; if both are absent, name + house match flags one; if only one exists, matching name + house flags one for manual review.
- **AC-32:** Potential duplicates are held for Event Admin review and never automatically inserted. Valid non-duplicate rows may proceed without cancelling the import. The Event Admin decides whether flagged rows proceed or are cancelled.
- **AC-33:** Event Admin can configure medal and participation points in one school-wide configuration; no event-level override can be created. Participation points may be disabled or set to zero.
- **AC-34:** Each published result retains the scoring configuration/version and outcome used. A configuration applies according to its approved effective date; historical results are not silently recalculated.
- **AC-35:** House ranking follows total points, gold, silver, bronze; exact ties use competition ranking, e.g. 1, 1, 3.
- **AC-36:** A user with both local roles still has one active school membership; the membership authorizes both roles in that school.
- **AC-37:** The first Event Admin is designated or verified by a Superadmin after school approval; activation is denied until verification completes.
- **AC-38:** Superadmin status alone cannot expose school operational data. Exceptional access requires explicit authorization, controlled scope, and audit; operating safeguards remain open under OD-08.
- **AC-39:** Assignment creation, permission changes, revocation, and cancellation are audited with actor and time.
- **AC-40:** Active Retention begins on the official end date of the school's Sports Day event and lasts 90 days.
- **AC-41:** An Event Admin may request up to two extensions before Active Retention expires; each adds up to seven days and requires Superadmin approval, for a maximum of 104 days.
- **AC-42:** An ordinary early-deletion request does not require Superadmin approval. When processed, it immediately revokes Event Admin access to affected data, begins 90-day Restricted Recovery, and records requester, time, and system action.
- **AC-43:** At normal Active Retention expiry, covered data enters 90-day Restricted Recovery and Event Admin access is revoked. At the end, covered data is permanently deleted, subject to separately retained records and legal/privacy requirements. Coverage, backup treatment, and exceptions remain open under OD-16.
- **AC-44:** The official competition-results archive is retained for a minimum of two years so official results and reports remain complete. It preserves school, event and event date, event/category, participant name, sports house, placing, medal, points, and relevant correction/withdrawal history. Retaining the participant name in this archive is an exception to the operational participant-data lifecycle; participant number and other operational participant fields are not retained there unless separately justified and approved. This retention does not authorize public disclosure: show_participant_names remains event-scoped and OFF by default, and public viewers receive only published, allowlisted projections under the existing Public/Unlisted rules.
- **AC-45:** Keep retention separate from participant-data lifecycle: Audit Trail records for a minimum of two years, Security Logs for 12 months, and routine Operational/Diagnostic Logs for 90 days. Incident/investigation records may be retained longer under a controlled process, and every extension is audited. Scoring configuration snapshots/version history and CSV import audit metadata remain separately identifiable; their durations and detailed handling remain open under OD-17. Technical logs must not contain original CSV content or unnecessary participant personal data. Hash-chain checkpoint hashes are separately protected; no retention duration is approved for them.
- **AC-46:** MVP event visibility has only Public and Unlisted states; Private is unavailable. Unlisted events are omitted from listings and their links are not access control.
- **AC-47:** Event Admins manage Scorekeeper assignments in-app and communicate manually through existing channels; automated email, push, and in-app notifications are not required in MVP.
- **AC-48:** CSV/PDF export workflows are absent from MVP scope, while published official results remain viewable through the public portal.
- **AC-49:** In an individual event, every medal-winning participant contributes the configured medal points to their house; multiple medal winners from one house in the same event each count.
- **AC-50:** A team medal result awards medal points to the winning house once per event result, independent of the number of team members.
- **AC-51:** Participation points are awarded only to participants who take part. Finished, DNF, and DSQ after starting are eligible; DNS, Absent, and Withdrawn before starting are not. Team participation points are awarded once per event result, not per member. Partial team participation remains open under OD-15.
- **AC-52:** Emergency Recovery requires an Event Admin request with reason and purpose and Superadmin approval/oversight. The requesting Event Admin receives restricted access only to the approved recovered data for up to three days; access expires automatically and Superadmin does not take over school management.
- **AC-53:** Before expiry, Event Admin may request up to two Emergency Recovery extensions of up to three days each. Each requires Superadmin approval, the approved scope remains unchanged, and the maximum total access period is nine days. Requests, approvals, revised expiry times, and access actions are audited. Operational enforcement remains open under OD-18.

### F-12 operational security and readiness criteria

These criteria record the approved F-12 baseline. Exact values and provider-specific settings remain open under OD-19 through OD-25; those gaps do not reduce the mandatory control outcomes.

- **AC-54:** All Superadmins and Event Admins must complete MFA before accessing any administrative function, in every environment and regardless of whether real participant data is present. MFA is recommended for Scorekeepers and not required for Public Viewers. Methods, enrollment, and recovery remain open under OD-19; they do not defer the MFA gate.
- **AC-55:** Privileged Superadmin and Event Admin accounts are reviewed on a documented recurring schedule; each review records its reviewer, date, and outcome. Accounts no longer required are suspended or deactivated. The exact review cadence remains open under OD-19.
- **AC-56:** Account suspension/deactivation, withdrawal of privileged access, password reset, or suspected compromise triggers active-session revocation; users can sign out of all sessions/devices. The chosen enforcement method remains open under OD-19.
- **AC-57:** Idle and absolute session limits are enforced; sensitive actions require re-authentication; security-relevant account/session events are recorded. Exact timeouts and sensitive-action list remain open under OD-19.
- **AC-58:** Each Audit Trail record is cryptographically linked to its predecessor; periodic verification uses separately protected checkpoint hashes; and any mismatch or verification failure is recorded in Security Logs and triggers a security alert. Security Logs also detect and record repeated failed logins; privileged activity meeting documented suspicious-activity criteria; cross-tenant access attempts; attempts to alter/delete Audit Trail records; temporary CSV deletion failures; and backup/restore failures. Exact suspicious-activity criteria and integrity-check settings remain open under OD-20.
- **AC-59:** Critical alerts reach responsible operational personnel, omit sensitive details, and trigger escalation/follow-up. Login, API, sensitive Server Action, CSV import, and public page/link access are rate-limited; where applicable, limits may consider account, IP address, and school context. Events triggering restrictions are logged. Exact thresholds, durations, and routing remain open under OD-20.
- **AC-60:** Incidents use the approved severities: Critical (cross-school exposure, compromised Superadmin, or Audit Trail manipulation); High (compromised Event Admin, backup/restore failure, or participant personal-data exposure); Medium (suspicious attempts, repeated import failures, or limited disruption without evidence of exposure).
- **AC-61:** Every incident follows Detection & Classification, Containment, Investigation & Evidence Preservation, System Recovery, and Documentation & Preventive Action. Critical and High incidents are escalated immediately; precise timing/on-call routing remains open under OD-20.
- **AC-62:** Automated periodic database backups and protection for inventoried important Supabase Storage files are maintained. Backups are encrypted and access-restricted; ordinary application users cannot modify or delete them. Record backup configuration and success/failure evidence. Frequency, retention, and region remain open under OD-21.
- **AC-63:** Documented restoration procedures and a Disaster Recovery Plan are exercised in at least one Restore Drill before pilot and on a recurring schedule selected under OD-21. Each drill verifies database and important Storage recovery, school/event/participant/result relationship consistency, restored RLS/access controls, and absence of cross-school exposure; the report records date, environment, measured recovery, results, issues, and corrective actions.
- **AC-64:** RPO/RTO targets are documented before pilot, based on verified infrastructure capabilities, and actual recovery is measured in drills. Numerical targets remain open under OD-21.
- **AC-65:** Scheduled maintenance and its expected service impact are documented, and notice is issued before a planned disruption under a published maintenance procedure. Urgent maintenance may address critical security issues and is recorded. No numerical uptime SLA is approved; notice timing and availability targets remain open under OD-22.
- **AC-66:** Availability is monitored against documented signals/thresholds; outages are recorded and investigated; post-outage checks cover login, event management, score entry, and result publication, with outcomes recorded. Monitoring method and thresholds remain open under OD-22.
- **AC-67:** Next.js, React, Supabase SDK, and other dependencies receive documented recurring security checks; vulnerabilities are tracked to resolution and critical patches are prioritized. Updates are tested in Development or Staging before Production, and documented rollback plans exist for changes meeting documented high-risk criteria. Check cadence, patch deadlines, and high-risk criteria remain open under OD-23.
- **AC-68:** Development and Production are separate. Staging is provided where feasible and does not replace Production isolation. API keys, credentials, and Storage are separate per environment.
- **AC-69:** Real participant data is not copied into Development or Staging; synthetic or de-identified data is used instead.
- **AC-70:** Every Production deployment passes the documented CI/CD validation checks. Migrations are version-controlled, tested in Development and Staging where available, and have a documented and verified recovery strategy. Production access is limited to named authorized roles/accounts in a maintained access roster; access is checked as part of release readiness. Exact pipeline/provider setup and roster remain open under OD-23/OD-24.
- **AC-71:** Before pilot, tests cover cross-tenant isolation, grants/RLS allow/deny behavior, RBAC for every user type, Supabase Storage, published-only Public Portal access, unauthorized-access protection, and documented findings/corrective actions.
- **AC-72:** All acceptance criteria pass before pilot. Critical and High vulnerabilities are resolved; Medium and Low findings have documented mitigations and assigned owners.
- **AC-73:** Pilot activation requires Superadmin approval of platform readiness and confirmation by each participating school's Event Admin of school operational readiness.
- **AC-74:** Before pilot, the Privacy Notice, Terms of Service, Data Retention Policy, Data Access & Disclosure Policy, and Incident & Breach Handling Procedure exist as controlled documents. Record the review date, reviewer roles, outcome, and follow-up actions for review with appropriate legal/privacy stakeholders. This criterion does not assert legal approval or PDPA compliance.
- **AC-75:** An official personal-data request channel supports copy/access, correction, and deletion requests subject to retention rules and legal obligations; requester identity/authority is checked, the responsible school or Meyyol handler is determined, and requests/decisions/actions are recorded.
- **AC-76:** A vendor register is maintained; vendors are assessed for security, privacy, operational suitability, data locations, purpose-limited access, outage/breach/discontinuation risks, and changes involving participant data.
- **AC-77:** Secret keys are not exposed in frontend code, browsers, public repositories, user-accessible source, logs, or error messages. Use secure secret management, separate credentials per environment, and restricted access; document revocation/rotation for compromised credentials and record/monitor critical credential events. The Supabase Service Role Key is never used in a browser or frontend.
- **AC-78:** HTTPS/TLS in transit and encryption at rest for database, backups, and Storage are required. Temporary CSV files are protected; keys are kept out of frontend/repositories, access is restricted, and key management/revocation/rotation and actual provider capabilities are verified before pilot. Algorithms, schedules, and provider capabilities remain open under OD-24.
- **AC-79:** Every data type documented for the MVP appears in the data-classification inventory in DATABASE.md and is assigned Public, Internal, or Restricted, or is explicitly marked Review with the uncertainty and closure owner recorded. Data becomes Public only through authorized publication; Restricted data is excluded from ordinary logs, errors, and public reports; storage, retention, transfer, disclosure, and deletion follow the inventory's rules.
- **AC-80:** Security policies/procedures have accountable owners, are reviewed at least annually and after significant system/infrastructure changes or serious incidents, and record document version, approval date, effective date, and reason for change. Security documents are stored in an access-controlled location; permissions are reviewed, privileged-access changes are recorded, and access is revoked when authorization is withdrawn. Repository access restrictions must be verified, not assumed.
- **AC-81:** Before system use, Superadmins, Event Admins, Scorekeepers, and all registered users receive the role-specific security guidance approved in F-12. Record the guide/version, audience, delivery date, and completion/acknowledgment evidence; issue updates after significant security changes.
- **AC-82:** A Security Owner and Backup Security Contact are appointed by name with documented responsibilities: the Security Owner coordinates policies, risk monitoring, vendor reviews, and incident response; the technical team implements controls, vulnerability fixes, and recovery; Event Admins handle security operations and access management in their schools. Record appointment and responsibility review when organizational arrangements change. One person may hold multiple duties if separation of duties and accountability are maintained. Named appointments remain an operational decision under OD-25.

## 10. Pilot objectives and readiness gates

The pilot should measure completion of school setup, participant entry and duplicate recovery, assignment management, score entry, publication, correction/withdrawal, public display, retention controls, and support needs. These are evaluation objectives, not approved numerical service-level targets.

Before real participant data is collected, satisfy AC-54 through AC-82 and resolve pilot blockers in OPEN_DECISIONS.md, including OD-09, OD-13, OD-16 through OD-25. Also resolve OD-07 and OD-08 before pilot acceptance. OD-14 and OD-15 must be resolved before the affected import or competition modes are implemented. This document records target behavior only; passing criteria requires implementation evidence and formal sign-off.
